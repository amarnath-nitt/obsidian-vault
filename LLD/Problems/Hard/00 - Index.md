## 🎯 How to choose

| Problem family | The hard part |
|----------------|---------------|
| **Money ledgers** (Splitwise, Stock Brokerage) | settle-up graph; no money created or lost |
| **Matching + pricing** (Uber, Food Delivery, Movie Tickets) | dispatch + surge; hold expiry |
| **Catalogues at scale** (Amazon, Spotify, Course Registration) | search + availability; waitlists |
| **Games + scoring** (Chess, Snake and Ladder, CricInfo) | move validation; live score fan-out |
